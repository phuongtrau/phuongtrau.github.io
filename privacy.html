Privacy Policy: Cosmo Uploader

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Cosmo Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Cosmo Curious YouTube channel to upload and manage the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions, set video titles, descriptions, visibility and publish times, and manage playlists on the owner's own channel.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data about anyone. The tool does not access other users' accounts or data. The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and is used only to make the requests listed above.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. YouTube's own terms apply to content on YouTube: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through youtube.com/@CosmoCurious-t8t.
